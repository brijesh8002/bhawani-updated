01Our Promise

We want you to love every Bhawani product. If something is not right, we will make it right. This policy explains when you can return an item, how to do it and when your refund will arrive.

02What Can Be Returned

Standard returns (7 days)

  • Unopened, unused products in their original, sealed packaging.
  • The return request must be raised within 7 days of delivery.

Defective, damaged or wrong items (15 days)

  • Leaking, broken or damaged products, or a different item from what you ordered.
  • Report it within 15 days of delivery with clear photos or a short video of the product and outer packaging.
  • You can choose a full refund or a replacement.

03What Cannot Be Returned

  • Opened, used or partly used products, unless they are defective or damaged.
  • Products without original packaging, labels or seals.
  • Items returned after the 7-day or 15-day window.
  • Free gifts and promotional items, which must be returned together with the order they came with.

For hygiene and safety reasons we cannot accept the return of cleaning products once the seal is broken.

04How to Request a Return

  1. Contact us. Message us on WhatsApp, email support@bhawani.in or use the contact form with your Order ID and, if applicable, photos.
  2. We review. Our team checks your request and replies within 24 hours.
  3. Pickup or drop-off. Once approved, we arrange a pickup or share return instructions. Please pack the item securely in its original packaging.
  4. Refund or replacement. After the item is received and inspected, we issue your refund or ship the replacement.

05Refunds

  • Refunds go to your original payment method. For Cash on Delivery orders we will ask for your UPI ID or bank details.
  • Approved refunds are usually processed within 5–7 business days after we receive and inspect the item. Your bank may take a few extra days to show the credit.
  • The shipping fee is refunded only when the return is due to our error (damaged, defective or wrong item).
  • The value of any free gift or extra discount that was tied to the order is adjusted if the return brings the order below the offer threshold.

06Replacements

If you choose a replacement for a defective or damaged item, we will ship it at no extra cost. If the product is out of stock, we will offer a refund or a similar product of your choice.

07Order Cancellation

You can cancel an order before it is dispatched by contacting us as soon as possible. Once an order has shipped it can no longer be cancelled, but you may be able to return it under this policy after delivery. Prepaid orders cancelled before dispatch are refunded in full.

08Damaged or Missing Parcels

Please check your parcel when it arrives. If the outer packaging looks tampered with or damaged, tell the courier and contact us right away with photos. If a parcel is lost in transit, we will resend it or refund you in full.
