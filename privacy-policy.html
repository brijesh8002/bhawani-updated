01Introduction

Bhawani (“we”, “us”) respects your privacy. This policy explains what personal information we collect when you use our website or buy our products, how we use it, and the choices you have. We process personal data in line with the Information Technology Act, 2000 and the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

02Information We Collect

Information you give us

  • Name, phone number, email address and delivery address when you order or contact us.
  • Order details, payment status and the messages you send us on WhatsApp, email or the contact form.
  • Reviews, feedback and newsletter sign-ups.

Information collected automatically

  • Device and browser type, approximate location, pages visited and time spent, to help us improve the website.
  • Cart and wishlist items, which are saved in your own browser’s local storage so they are still there when you return.

We do not collect or store your card or UPI credentials; these are handled directly by payment providers.

03How We Use Your Information

  • To process, deliver and support your orders, returns and refunds.
  • To reply to your questions and complaints.
  • To send order updates by SMS, email or WhatsApp.
  • To send offers and news, only if you have subscribed — you can opt out at any time.
  • To keep the website secure, prevent fraud and meet legal obligations.
  • To understand how the website is used and make it better.

04Consent

By placing an order, contacting us or subscribing to our newsletter, you consent to us processing your information for the purposes described here. You can withdraw consent at any time by writing to support@bhawani.in; withdrawal does not affect processing already done, and some information may still need to be kept to meet legal or accounting requirements.

05Cookies & Local Storage

We use local storage and, where applicable, cookies to remember your cart, wishlist and preferences, and to understand website traffic. You can clear or block them in your browser settings, but parts of the website, such as the cart, may then not work as expected.

06Who We Share Information With

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with trusted partners who help us serve you, and only as much as they need:

  • Courier and logistics partners — to deliver your order.
  • Payment providers — to process payments and refunds.
  • Communication services such as WhatsApp, SMS and email providers.
  • Authorities — when required by law or to protect our rights and users.

07How Long We Keep Information

We keep your information only for as long as needed to fulfil the purposes above, resolve disputes and comply with tax and legal requirements. After that it is deleted or anonymised.

08Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational safeguards to protect your information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security, but we work to protect your data and will act promptly if an issue arises.

09Your Rights

You may ask us to:

  • Tell you what personal information we hold about you.
  • Correct or update inaccurate information.
  • Delete your information, subject to legal retention requirements.
  • Stop marketing messages (use the unsubscribe option or write to us).

Write to support@bhawani.in and we will respond within a reasonable time. If you are not satisfied, you may also approach the Data Protection Board of India as provided under the DPDP Act, 2023.

10Children

Our website is not directed at children under 18, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information. If you believe a child has given us personal data, please contact us and we will delete it.

11Third-Party Links

Our website may link to other websites and social networks. We are not responsible for their content or privacy practices, so please read their policies.

12Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The “last updated” date above shows the latest version, and significant changes will be highlighted on the website.
