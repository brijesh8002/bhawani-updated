Born from a Simple Belief

Bhawani was founded with one simple belief: your home deserves better than harsh chemicals. We started in 2022 with a small team of passionate scientists, eco-warriors, and parents who wanted safer cleaning products for Indian households.

Today, Bhawani is trusted by over 10 lakh families across India — from metros to tier-2 cities — who believe that a clean home doesn't have to come at the cost of their family's health or the planet's future.

Every product we make is plant-based, dermatologically tested, 0% bleach, and cruelty-free. Because we believe clean should never be complicated.

Our Values

What drives every decision we make at Bhawani

🌿
Plant-Based First
Every formula starts with nature. We use plant-derived surfactants and essential oils instead of harsh synthetics.
🔬
Science-Backed
All our products are tested in certified labs and dermatologically approved for family safety.
🌍
Planet Responsible
Biodegradable formulas, recyclable packaging, and a commitment to reducing our carbon footprint.
🐰
Cruelty Free
We have never and will never test on animals. Every product is certified cruelty-free.
🇮🇳
Made in India
Proudly manufactured in India, supporting local communities and reducing import footprint.
💚
Family Safe
Safe for kids, pets, and sensitive skin. No harsh bleach, no toxic fumes, no compromises.
