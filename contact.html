Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions

How long does delivery take? +
Most orders are delivered within 3–5 business days. Express delivery (1–2 days) is available in select metro cities. You'll receive a tracking link via SMS and email once your order ships.
Are Bhawani products safe for kids and pets? +
Yes! All our products are plant-based, 0% bleach, and dermatologically tested. They're safe to use around children and pets when used as directed. We recommend keeping products out of reach of small children.
What is your return policy? +
We offer a 7-day hassle-free return policy on unopened products. For defective or damaged items, we offer a full refund or replacement within 15 days of delivery. Contact our support team to initiate a return.
Do you offer bulk / B2B orders? +
Yes! We offer special pricing for bulk orders (hotels, housing societies, offices, etc.). Contact us via WhatsApp or email at b2b@bhawani.in with your requirements and we'll get back within 24 hours.
Is free shipping available? +
Yes! We offer free shipping on all orders above ₹199. For orders below ₹199, a nominal shipping fee of ₹49 is charged.
Are Bhawani products cruelty-free? +
Absolutely. We are proudly cruelty-free. We never test our products on animals and never will. Our formulas are tested using certified in-vitro methods approved by international standards.
